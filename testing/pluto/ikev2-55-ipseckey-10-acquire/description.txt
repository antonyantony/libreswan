Regression test for issue #3042. The trigger must be a ping to a
hostname that resolves to more than one IP address (here,
east-private.testing.libreswan.org has both an A and an AAAA
record) -- glibc's getaddrinfo()/connect() replaces the dport ping
sets, 1025, for its route-probe socket with 0 whenever a
name resolves to multiple addresses. Pluto used to treat
dport=0 acquire as fatal since commit
29b79c912774 ("ip_packet: inline pexpect_packet() into packet_from_raw()")
and drop the acquire, instead of initiating. If the ping continues,
 the kernel's xfrm_acq_expires (default 30 seconds), would establish
the connection.
